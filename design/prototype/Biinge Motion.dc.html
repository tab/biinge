Rules
Things grow out of what was clicked, tapped or focused, and go back to it.
Standard does most of the work. Focus is only for TV focus and icon pops.
Exits run at about 70 % of the entrance time.
A new input always interrupts and retargets the running animation. Nothing blocks clicks or the remote.
Only the TV hero, skeleton shimmer and spinners move on their own.
A pressed button shows its spinner at once. Page skeletons wait 300 ms and playback spinners 500 ms. Once shown, either stays at least 500 ms so it never flickers.
Want to Watch and Watched update instantly and sync in the background. If the sync fails, the change reverts and a toast explains why. These buttons never show a spinner.
During playback, only the controls, subtitles and the next-episode morph move over the video.
Avoid
Bounce or springy overshoot on Mac windows, menus and sheets.
Parallax on scroll and tilt effects on Mac and iPhone posters.
Animating lists and grids on first load beyond the poster fade-in.
Page-turn, flip or zoom-through transitions between sidebar sections. Sidebar sections switch instantly.
Motion that carries meaning on its own. Every state change also shows in the final frame.
Full-screen spinners where a skeleton can show the layout, and buttons that change width while they load.
