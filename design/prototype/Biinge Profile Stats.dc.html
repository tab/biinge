Profile, Appearance, Statistics
Rebuilt from the Design System · Statistics components · iOS screens
1Profile: one account card holds the Biinge account and the Jellyfin connection. Statistics is a menu row. Integrations is gone.
2Appearance: three preview tiles and a live preview that re-themes as you pick. Posters and video stay dark in every theme.
3Statistics: pill filter, a headline total with the change against the previous period, tap a bar for its breakdown, highlights, category cards with posters, Most watched.
4New data colours for charts only: Movies violet, TV cyan, Games amber. Same lightness and chroma, so no category reads louder.
5macOS and Apple TV get Statistics for Movies and TV. Games stay on iPhone. Appearance stays iPhone-only; Mac and TV follow the system, as in the brief.
6Week and Month are the last 7 and 30 days, so a new month never opens on an empty chart.
