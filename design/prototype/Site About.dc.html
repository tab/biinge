About

One place for what you want to watch and what you can play.

Biinge is an open-source, self-hosted app for iPhone, Mac and Apple TV. It began as a tracker for movies, TV shows and games. Today it also plays the library on your own server, so the list and the play button finally live together.

Every app signs in to both. Biinge supplies the address of your Jellyfin server, so you only enter a username and password. When you mark something watched, both servers are updated, so they always agree.

Progress sets the state

Movies move from Want to Watched. TV shows add Watching in between, and Biinge works out where a show belongs from the episodes you mark. Games use Want, Playing and Played, with every move under your control.

Want
Movies, shows and games you plan to start. Each keeps its own list.
Watching
Shows move here after the first episode you mark or finish. Games use Playing.
Watched
Finished movies and ended shows. Games use Played. Their runtime counts toward your totals.

Your library stays current

Release dates change and finished shows sometimes return. A background worker refreshes movies and shows from TMDB, so corrected details reach your lists without you reopening every title. If an ended show is renewed, it returns to Watching.

Your servers, your data

There is no hosted Biinge service. You run the API next to your Jellyfin server and keep your lists and watch history in your own PostgreSQL database. There is no advertising and nothing to subscribe to.

Your server asks TMDB for movie and TV metadata and IGDB for games. Optional Sentry crash reporting stays off until you configure it.

How it’s built

A Go REST API owns lists, episode progress, Up Next and statistics, and keeps provider credentials on your server instead of in the apps. The iPhone, Mac and Apple TV apps share one SwiftUI codebase and play through Apple’s own video engine.
