Biinge for Apple TV

Relax and watch.

Biinge on the big screen. Large artwork, a focus you can always see, and only a few clicks between the couch and the opening scene.

Home

One swipe from playing.

A featured title on top, then Continue Watching, Next Up and what was just added, as large rows you move through with the remote. Select a card and it plays.

Focus

A focus you can follow from across the room.

The focused poster lifts and catches the light, and its title drops clear. Go back from a details page and focus is where you left it.

TV Shows

The next episode is always first in line.

Seasons and episodes share one screen, with watched marks and runtimes. When the credits roll, the next episode counts down beside the picture.

My Lists on the couch
Want to Watch, Watching and Watched as three poster rows. Select a title to play it or see where it stands.
Audio and subtitles from the remote
Swipe down while playing to change tracks or subtitle style. Your server’s default languages are already set.

Everything on the Siri Remote.

No hover, no deep menus and as little typing as possible. When you do need to type, use the on-screen keyboard or your iPhone.

What you need
tv
An Apple TV on tvOS 26
dns
A Biinge server for your account, lists and progress
video_library
A Jellyfin server, 12.3 or later, for your library and playback
How to install
Biinge isn’t on the App Store. Build the Apple TV app with Xcode and install it from your Mac over the network. Sign in once with your Biinge account and your Jellyfin user.
