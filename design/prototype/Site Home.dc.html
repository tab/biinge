Lists and library

Your lists and your library, side by side.

Lists hold everything you mean to see. The library holds everything you can play tonight. One switch at the top of Movies and TV moves between them.

Want, Watching and Watched
Add any movie or show from the catalog, whether you own it yet or not. Pin the ones that matter to the top.
Library
Continue Watching, Recently Added and every movie and show on your Jellyfin server.
Play from anywhere
A title on your server shows Play in its details, whether you found it in a list, in search or on a cast member’s page.
TV progress

Tick an episode. The show keeps up.

Mark one episode, a season or the whole show, and Biinge moves the show between Want, Watching and Watched. Finish an episode in the player and it’s ticked for you.

Watched means watched everywhere
A mark made in any app is written to Jellyfin too, so the two always agree.
Correct it anytime
Untick an episode and the show’s progress and state are worked out again.
Ready for new seasons
A returning series stays in Watching when you’re caught up, with the next season waiting.

Playback

A native player that gets out of the way.

Every app plays with Apple’s own video engine, streamed from your server. Controls fade after three seconds, and when the credits roll the next episode is already counting down.

Audio and subtitles in one panel
Every track with its language and format. Subtitle size, background and timing sit one step in.
Next episode
At the credits the video moves aside and keeps playing while the next episode counts down.
Your languages by default
Audio and subtitles start in the languages you set on your server, and a series can keep the tracks you pick.
