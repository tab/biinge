Biinge for Mac

Browse and watch.

A media center for your library, built for the Mac. A sidebar for everything, rich details when you want them, and a player that lives in a window or fills the screen.

Home

Home answers what’s next.

Continue Watching, Next Up, Recently Added and your collections, under a featured title you can play straight away. You never have to go looking for the episode you stopped in.

TV Shows

Every season at a glance.

Series pages show overall progress, a season picker and every episode with its runtime, progress and watched mark. The next unwatched episode is the one Play starts.

Player

A player that fits the Mac.

Play in a window or full screen. Audio and subtitles share one panel, speed runs from 0.5× to 2×, and the next episode counts down at the credits.

checkSubtitle size, background, position and timing, set once for every title.
checkKeep the tracks you pick for the rest of a series.
My Lists in the sidebar
Want to Watch, Watching and Watched from Biinge, each with its count. Posters on your server show their resume bar, and a double-click plays them.
Collections and search
Browse the collections you keep in Jellyfin. Search the whole catalog for movies, shows and people from anywhere in the app.

Made for the keyboard.

A Mac app should work without reaching for the trackpad. Right-click any poster for its actions, and collapse the sidebar when you want the room.

What you need
laptop_mac
A Mac on macOS 26
dns
A Biinge server for your account, lists and progress
video_library
A Jellyfin server, 12.3 or later, for your library and playback
How to install
Biinge isn’t on the App Store. Build the Mac app from the repository with Xcode. Sign in with your Biinge account, then with your Jellyfin user. Biinge finds your server for you.
