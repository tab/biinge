Biinge for iPhone

Track, discover and play.

The Biinge tracker, restyled, with your library built in. Your lists, episode progress, games and statistics work as before, and anything on your server plays without leaving the app.

Movies and TV

Lists and library under one switch.

Movies has Want, Watched and Library. TV adds Watching. Library holds Continue Watching, Recently Added and everything on your Jellyfin server, with a sort for the full grid.

checkPosters you can play show their resume bar, even in your lists.
checkPins, progress pies and NEW badges match the Mac and Apple TV.
Details

Play or Resume, right from the details.

A title on your server gets a white Play or Resume button. Episodes play from their own sheet, and playback turns sideways to fill the screen.

checkWatched and Want sit beside Play as round buttons.
checkFinish an episode and it’s ticked in Biinge and on your server.
Up Next

What you’re watching, then what’s coming.

Up Next opens with Continue Watching and the next episode of every show you’ve started. New episodes and release days for everything you track follow underneath.

Only on iPhone

Games and statistics stay.

Games
Want, Playing and Played, with IGDB’s time to beat for the main story and the 100% run. Pin an upcoming game and it shows in Up Next on release day.
Statistics
Every movie and episode adds its runtime when you mark it. Compare movies and TV by week, month, year or all time.

What you need
smartphone
An iPhone on iOS 26
dns
A Biinge server for your account, lists and progress
video_library
A Jellyfin server, 12.3 or later, for your library and playback
How to install
Biinge isn’t on the App Store. Build it from the repository with Xcode and install it on your iPhone from your Mac. Sign in with your Biinge account, then with your Jellyfin user.
